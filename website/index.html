    BEFORE YOU START

    A few
    answers.

    Where can I try CyDaw?

    The alpha offered here is for Windows x64. Check the release notes on GitHub for requirements and available downloads.

    Is this a finished release?

    CyDaw is an alpha in active development. Try it with copies of your projects and keep backups. Known limits are described in the manual and release notes.

    Can I export my own plugins?

    Plugin workshop exports VST3, CLAP and LV2. Required compilers and SDKs depend on the target. The editable CyDaw device format lets you share graphs, interfaces and presets with another CyDaw installation.

    Which external plugins can I use?

    VST3, CLAP, LV2 and JSFX effects are hosted on audio tracks in this version. See the manual for format-specific limits and setup.

    Does the manual work offline?

    Yes. The bundled reader includes its images, diagrams and search. This showcase also links to the complete web edition.
